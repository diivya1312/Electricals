⚡ OUR SYSTEMATIC APPROACH

FROM IDEA
TO ILLUMINATION.

01

SITE VISIT

Detailed on-site inspection, measurement, electrical load calculation, and architectural assessment.

02

REQUIREMENT ANALYSIS

Understanding power distribution needs, aesthetic lighting goals, budget criteria, and timeline.

03

PLANNING

Circuit design, wire gauge selection, DB panel layout, breaker specifications, and lighting plan.

04

INSTALLATION

Precision wall chasing, concealed conduit piping, heavy wire pulling, and DB box dressing.

05

TESTING

Rigorous insulation resistance testing, earth pit verification, phase load balancing, and thermal checks.

06

HANDOVER

Final fixture fitting, illumination turn-on test, client walkthrough, and safety certification.
